Design kit

Every component in the Signpost direction, in every state it has. Everything here is drawn from the tokens in style.css.

Frames

The frame is the whole argument. It comes in three weights and never in any other.

2px panel

Panels, rows, tiles, fields, buttons.

3px emphasis

The sign-in frame, a field with an error, the chosen radio tile.

4px alert

Alert panels and the error summary.

Buttons

Pressing any button moves it 2px and collapses its shadow to 1px. A busy button is held down and reads "Sending…". Primary buttons are 56px tall, 64px on a phone.

Links

A link in prose looks like this: open the requests queue. Links are underlined 2px in the strong crest colour, and light up on hover. In a list row the title is the link, padded to a 44px target.

Spacing

A 10px base with one sub-step: 4 · 10 · 20 · 30 · 40 · 60 · 80. Panel padding is 20, panel to panel 20, section to section 40.

Focus ring

A 4px ring with a 2px offset, the thickest of the five directions, because a thin ring would be lost against 2px frames. It is never removed.
