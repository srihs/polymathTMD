# v5 — Tasks First

**Idea:** the app is organised around what people came to do, not around data. Home opens with six big verb tiles (Receive stock, Give out items, Find an item, Count stock, Add a new item, See low stock). A compact "store today" strip and the "Needs attention" list sit beneath them, with one-tap Reorder/Receive. Every job is a short guided step: a stepper with a live "New total will be…" preview, a 1 Details → 2 Stock → 3 Price step indicator that tracks progress and errors, and a success toast with Undo.

**Fonts:** Nunito (rounded headings, buttons), Nunito Sans (body, 16–17px), and Cinzel (classical caps) only for the "Polymath College" wordmark beside the crest. **Palette:** a bright white canvas with a faint crest-purple tint (`#F9F4FA`) on the sidebar. Crest purple `#722A82` is used only for primary actions, the current nav item and the duotone icons. The status colours (green, amber, red, blue) appear only on stock states, always with an icon and a word. The chart pair (`#8C47A0` in / `#16A3AD` out) passed the dataviz palette validator.

**Why it suits the users:** staff never have to translate a goal into a menu. They tap the verb. Labels and help text are written in everyday words, and every field says what to type. Errors say how to fix the problem and keep what was typed. Archiving explains that nothing is deleted. Targets are 44–56px, and on phones a bottom tab bar (Home · Find · **Receive** · Give out · Me) keeps the main job under the thumb.
